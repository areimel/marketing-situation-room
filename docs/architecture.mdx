title: "Agency workspace design"
description: "How the roster, briefing, and signals fit together in the agency dashboard."
---

## Start with an account

An agency handles several clients at once. The roster answers which account the analyst is reviewing. Keep that selection visible when presenting the account's briefing and signals so a user switching clients can tell whose information they are reading.

## Read the briefing alongside its signals

The briefing belongs to the selected client. Alongside it, the headlines view presents shared industry coverage from Google News. Switching accounts changes the briefing context while the feed continues to cover the same industry topics. Label that distinction so an analyst can tell whether a headline mentions their client.

## Distinguish local observations from provider data

The agency app starts with local client data. Marketing API contracts also exist for weather, events, mentions, and demand trends. Describe a signal as provider-backed only when its path from a provider through a handler to the displayed observation has been implemented.
