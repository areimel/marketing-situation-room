## Design context

Marketing Situation Room serves agency teams monitoring several client accounts. The roster should let an account manager scan the current briefs and select a client. Keep the selected account visible when editing a brief, reviewing research or downloading a report.

Use the shared workspace styles for forms and cards. Preserve readable labels and keyboard focus, let long client names wrap, and stack the roster and workspace on narrow screens. Distinguish fictional portfolio data, client-entered observations and live industry headlines wherever their source affects interpretation.
